Scope reported by a sender is data. The pilot isolates payloads and does not implement native A2A transport, discovery or lifecycle. [Schema](https://github.com/TAM-Hiding/Standard-Interlog/blob/8c513629c67f44776b5c2b7a8ce9ec85345d488a/schemas/sil-experimental.schema.json) · [Adapters](https://github.com/TAM-Hiding/Standard-Interlog/blob/8c513629c67f44776b5c2b7a8ce9ec85345d488a/sil_pilot/formats.py) · [Backend](https://github.com/TAM-Hiding/Standard-Interlog/blob/8c513629c67f44776b5c2b7a8ce9ec85345d488a/sil_pilot/backend.py)

## Assumptions requiring confirmation before live operation

Nexus starts with one trusted human principal and one trusted Linux coordinator. Host hardening, always-on service, remote worker trust, production budgets, repository credential policy and corpus retention defaults have no confirmed historical agreement here. Assume local fixtures and no remote operations for NX-002. CSI from WO-001 and later SIL are related research context. No inspected decision equates their schemas or binds Nexus to either. NX-001 architecture choices are new proposals.
